MSc Zoology Student | Neurodegeneration, Mitochondrial Biology & Animal Behaviour

Thanks for stopping by my little corner of the internet!

I’m an MSc Zoology student developing research experience in neurodegeneration, mitochondrial biology and animal behaviour. I am particularly interested in how mitochondrial dysfunction and cellular stress contribute to age-related neurological disease.

My current research training at the Molecular Neurogerontology Laboratory, Bharathidasan University involves Alzheimer’s disease-related work using a rat model, with hands-on exposure to mitochondrial isolation and biochemical assessment of mitochondrial function.

My earlier work spans animal behaviour, organismal biology and experimental zoology, including a behavioural study of Mahseer fish at the National Institute of Advanced Studies and research training through the NIUS Biology Programme.
